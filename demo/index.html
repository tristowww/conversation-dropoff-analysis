What would I do with this?

Use the flagged turns to choose a review window. Read the questions and responses around that window, check whether the conversation was naturally ending, and compare the pattern across groups before changing an interview.

These methods all use response length. Agreement between them is not a confidence score. Even a noisy flat synthetic series produces a flagged turn, so a result needs context and separate human review.

Why the alternative views matter

  • First responses can be short acknowledgments.
  • Final responses can be brief because the interview is complete.
  • Later turns include fewer people. The same-conversation view holds the group fixed through turn 10, but represents longer interviews only.

No human engagement ratings were collected for this analysis. The public-data run tests the workflow and its sensitivity, not whether it correctly identifies disengagement.
